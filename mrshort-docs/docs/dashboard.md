# Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is the central hub for managing your MRShort account. Here's everything you need to know.

## Main Dashboard Sections

### Quick Shortener

The input field at the bottom of your dashboard lets you quickly shorten URLs:

1. Paste your URL
2. Click **Shorten Now**
3. Your link is ready!

### Statistics Cards

At the top, you'll see key metrics:

| Card | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Active Today** | Days you've been active |
| **Longest Streak** | Your longest consecutive active days |
| **Daily Challenges** | Today's tasks and rewards |
| **Weekly Competition** | Your ranking among other users |

### Performance Chart

The chart shows your click and earnings trends over time. You can:
- Toggle between **Clicks** and **Earnings**
- View data for different time periods
- Identify your best performing days

### Recently Shortened Links

Quick access to your most recent links with:
- Short URL
- Original URL
- Click count
- Quick copy button

## Navigation Sidebar

Access all features from the sidebar:

- **Dashboard** - Main overview
- **Links** - Manage all your links
- **Withdrawals** - Request payouts
- **Tools** - Mass shortener, API, etc.
- **Campaigns** - Advertising features
- **Referrals** - Your referral program
- **Reports** - Detailed analytics
- **Gamification** - Rewards and achievements
- **Help & Support** - Documentation and contact

## Notifications

Click the bell icon to see:
- System announcements
- Payment updates
- Achievement unlocks
- Important alerts

## Account Menu

Access your profile settings by clicking your name/avatar:
- View balance
- Quick settings access
- Logout
