# Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Secure your account with two-factor authentication.

## What is 2FA?

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security. Even if someone knows your password, they can't access your account without your phone.

## Setting Up 2FA

### Step 1: Download an Authenticator App

Install one of these apps on your phone:
- **Google Authenticator** (Android/iOS)
- **Authy** (Android/iOS/Desktop)
- **Microsoft Authenticator** (Android/iOS)

### Step 2: Enable 2FA

1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **Enable 2FA**
3. A QR code will appear

### Step 3: Scan the QR Code

1. Open your authenticator app
2. Tap **Add account** or **+**
3. Select **Scan QR code**
4. Point your camera at the screen

### Step 4: Verify

1. Enter the 6-digit code from your app
2. Click **Verify**
3. 2FA is now enabled!

### Step 5: Save Backup Codes

:::warning Important!
Save your backup codes in a safe place. You'll need them if you lose access to your phone.
:::

Backup codes:
- Are one-time use only
- Allow login without your phone
- Should be stored securely (password manager, printed copy)

## Logging In with 2FA

1. Enter your email and password as usual
2. When prompted, enter the 6-digit code from your app
3. Click **Verify**

:::tip
Codes refresh every 30 seconds. If a code fails, wait for a new one.
:::

## Disabling 2FA

1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **Disable 2FA**
3. Enter your current 2FA code
4. Confirm with your password

## Lost Phone / Can't Access 2FA?

### Use Backup Codes

1. On the 2FA prompt, click **Use backup code**
2. Enter one of your saved backup codes
3. Access your account and set up new 2FA

### Contact Support

If you've lost both your phone and backup codes:
1. Go to **Contact Us**
2. Submit a ticket explaining the situation
3. We'll verify your identity and help recover your account

## FAQ

### What if my code isn't working?
- Make sure your phone's time is synced correctly
- Wait for a new code (they change every 30 seconds)
- Try manually entering the setup key instead of scanning QR

### Can I use 2FA on multiple devices?
Yes, scan the QR code on multiple authenticator apps during setup.

### Is 2FA required?
Not required, but strongly recommended for account security.
