# Gamification

Earn extra rewards through MRShort's gamification features!

## Daily Spin

Spin the wheel once every 24 hours to win:
- 💰 Bonus credits
- 🎁 Mystery boxes
- ⭐ XP points
- 🎫 Special items

## Achievements

Complete tasks to unlock achievements and earn rewards:

| Achievement | Requirement | Reward |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| First Link | Create your first link | 10 XP |
| Rising Star | Get 100 clicks | 50 XP |
| Influencer | Get 1,000 clicks | 200 XP |
| Referral Master | Refer 5 users | 500 XP |

## VIP Levels

Level up by earning XP:

- **Bronze** - Starting level
- **Silver** - Better CPM rates
- **Gold** - Priority support
- **Diamond** - Exclusive features

## Weekly Competition

Compete with other users for the top spots on the leaderboard. Top performers win bonus rewards!

## Teams

Join or create a team to:
- Compete in team challenges
- Share strategies
- Earn team bonuses

## Mystery Boxes

Open mystery boxes to win random prizes:
- Credits
- XP boosts
- Rare items
- Special bonuses

:::tip
Check your dashboard daily to claim all available rewards!
:::
