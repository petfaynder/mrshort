# Getting Started

Learn how to create your first shortened link and start earning money with MRShort.

## Step 1: Create an Account

1. Visit [mrshort.io](https://mrshort.io)
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top right corner
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address

## Step 2: Shorten Your First Link

1. After logging in, you'll see the **Quick Shortener** on your dashboard
2. Paste any URL you want to shorten
3. (Optional) Customize your link alias
4. Click **Shorten**
5. Copy your new short link and share it!

## Step 3: Share and Earn

When someone clicks your shortened link, they'll see an advertisement before being redirected to the destination. You earn money for each valid click.

### Tips for More Earnings

- Share links on social media platforms
- Create engaging content around your links
- Use the **Mass Shortener** for bulk link creation
- Enable **Telegram Traffic Bonus** for extra earnings

## Understanding Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Today's Clicks** | Clicks received today |
| **Today's Earnings** | Money earned today |
| **Total Balance** | Your available balance for withdrawal |
| **Performance Chart** | Visual representation of your clicks over time |
