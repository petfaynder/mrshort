# Earnings & Payments

Everything you need to know about earning money and withdrawing your balance.

## How Earnings Work

You earn money for every **valid click** on your shortened links. Rates vary based on:

- **Country** - Tier 1 countries (US, UK, etc.) pay higher rates
- **Device** - Desktop vs mobile traffic
- **Ad campaigns** - Active campaigns may boost rates

### What Counts as a Valid Click?

A click is counted when:
- ✅ Visitor is unique (not a repeat within 24 hours)
- ✅ Visitor completes the ad viewing process
- ✅ Traffic is from a real person (not bots)

### What Doesn't Count?

- ❌ Self-clicks (clicking your own links)
- ❌ Bot or proxy traffic
- ❌ Multiple clicks from the same IP within 24 hours
- ❌ Invalid or suspicious traffic

## CPM Rates

CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the amount you earn per 1,000 valid clicks.

:::tip Check Current Rates
View the latest CPM rates in your dashboard under **Reports → CPM Rates**
:::

## Withdrawing Your Earnings

### Minimum Withdrawal

The minimum withdrawal amount is **$5.00** (may vary by payment method).

### Available Payment Methods

- PayPal
- Bank Transfer
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT)
- Payoneer

### Processing Time

- **PayPal**: 1-3 business days
- **Crypto**: Within 24 hours
- **Bank Transfer**: 3-5 business days

## Boosting Your Earnings

1. **Referral Program** - Earn 10% of your referrals' earnings
2. **Daily Spin** - Win bonus credits daily
3. **Achievements** - Complete tasks for rewards
4. **VIP Status** - Higher levels = better rates
