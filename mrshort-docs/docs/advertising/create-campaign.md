# Create an Advertising Campaign

Promote your links or website with MRShort advertising.

## Campaign Types

### Pop-Under Ads
Your ad appears in a new tab when users click shortened links.

### Banner Ads
Display banner ads on the redirect pages.

### Interstitial Ads
Full-page ads shown before the destination.

## Creating a Campaign

### Step 1: Access Campaigns

1. Go to **Campaigns** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create New Campaign**

### Step 2: Campaign Details

Fill in basic information:
- **Campaign Name** - Internal name for tracking
- **Target URL** - Where users go when they click your ad
- **Campaign Type** - Pop-under, banner, or interstitial

### Step 3: Budget & Bidding

Set your spending limits:
- **Daily Budget** - Maximum spend per day
- **Total Budget** - Overall campaign limit
- **Bid Amount** - How much you pay per view/click

### Step 4: Targeting (Optional)

Narrow your audience:
- **Countries** - Target specific regions
- **Devices** - Desktop, mobile, or both
- **Operating Systems** - Windows, iOS, Android, etc.
- **Browsers** - Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.

### Step 5: Schedule

Choose when your ads run:
- **Start Date** - When to begin
- **End Date** - When to stop (optional)
- **Time of Day** - Specific hours (optional)

### Step 6: Review & Launch

1. Review all settings
2. Add funds if needed
3. Click **Launch Campaign**

## Campaign Dashboard

After creating a campaign, monitor performance:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Impressions | Times your ad was shown |
| Clicks | Times users clicked your ad |
| CTR | Click-through rate (clicks/impressions) |
| Spend | Total amount spent |
| Remaining | Budget left |

## Managing Campaigns

### Pause/Resume
Toggle campaign status without deleting.

### Edit
Modify targeting, budget, and other settings.

### Duplicate
Create a copy for A/B testing.

### Delete
Permanently remove a campaign.

## Tips for Success

1. **Start small** - Test with small budget first
2. **Target precisely** - Better targeting = better ROI
3. **A/B test** - Try different ads and landing pages
4. **Monitor daily** - Adjust based on performance
5. **Quality landing pages** - Good UX improves conversions
