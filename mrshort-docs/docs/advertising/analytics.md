# Campaign Analytics

Track and optimize your advertising campaigns.

## Accessing Analytics

1. Go to **Campaigns**
2. Click on a campaign name
3. View the **Analytics** tab

## Key Metrics

### Impressions
Number of times your ad was displayed.

### Clicks
Number of times users clicked on your ad.

### CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.

```
CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100
```

**Good CTR benchmarks:**
- Pop-under: 0.5-2%
- Banner: 0.1-0.5%
- Interstitial: 1-5%

### Spend
Total amount spent on the campaign.

### CPC (Cost Per Click)
Average cost for each click.

```
CPC = Total Spend / Total Clicks
```

### Remaining Budget
How much budget is left.

## Analytics Dashboard

### Overview Cards
Quick stats at the top:
- Today's impressions
- Today's clicks
- Total spend
- Remaining budget

### Performance Chart
Visual timeline showing:
- Daily impressions trend
- Daily clicks trend
- Toggle between metrics

### Geographic Breakdown
See where your traffic comes from:
- Top countries by impressions
- Click rates by country
- Spend by region

### Device Breakdown
Performance by device type:
- Desktop vs mobile
- Best performing devices
- Adjust targeting based on data

## Date Filters

Analyze different time periods:
- Today
- Yesterday
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom range

## Exporting Data

Download your analytics:
1. Click **Export**
2. Choose format (CSV, Excel)
3. Select date range
4. Download

## Optimization Tips

Based on your analytics:

### Low CTR?
- Improve ad creative
- Check landing page relevance
- Adjust targeting

### High CPC?
- Lower your bid
- Target less competitive regions
- Improve ad quality

### Not enough impressions?
- Increase budget
- Broaden targeting
- Raise bid amount

### Good CTR but poor conversions?
- Check landing page
- Ensure targeting matches audience
- Verify tracking is working
