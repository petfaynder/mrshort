## What is MRShort?

MRShort is a link shortening platform that pays you for every click on your shortened links. Simply create short links, share them, and earn money when people click on them.

### Key Features

- **High CPM Rates** - Earn competitive rates for worldwide traffic
- **Real-time Analytics** - Track your clicks, earnings, and audience
- **API Access** - Integrate link shortening into your workflow
- **Fast Payouts** - Multiple withdrawal options available
- **Gamification** - Daily spins, achievements, and bonuses
