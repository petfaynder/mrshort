# Quick Start Guide

Get started with MRShort in under 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Go to [mrshort.io](https://mrshort.io)
2. Click **Register** 
3. Fill in your details:
   - Username
   - Email
   - Password
4. Verify your email

## Step 2: Create Your First Link

Once logged in, you'll see the **Quick Shortener** on your dashboard:

1. Paste any URL into the input field
2. Click **Shorten Now**
3. Copy your new short link!

```
Original: https://example.com/very-long-url-with-parameters?id=12345
Shortened: https://mrshort.io/abc123
```

## Step 3: Share & Earn

Share your short link anywhere:
- Social media (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram)
- YouTube video descriptions
- Blog posts
- Forums
- Messaging apps

:::tip Pro Tip
The more targeted your audience, the higher your click-through rate!
:::

## Step 4: Track Your Earnings

Check your **Dashboard** to see:
- Real-time click count
- Today's earnings
- Total balance

## Step 5: Withdraw

Once you reach the minimum threshold:
1. Go to **Withdrawals**
2. Choose your payment method
3. Enter the amount
4. Submit withdrawal request
