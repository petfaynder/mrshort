# Referral Program

Earn passive income by referring new users to MRShort!

## How It Works

1. **Share your referral link** with friends and followers
2. **They sign up** using your link
3. **You earn 10%** of their earnings forever!

:::info Lifetime Commission
Your referral earnings continue as long as your referrals are active. There's no time limit!
:::

## Finding Your Referral Link

1. Go to **Referrals** in the sidebar
2. Copy your unique referral link
3. Share it anywhere!

Your referral link format:
```
https://mrshort.io/register?ref=YOUR_USERNAME
```

## Best Practices

### Where to Share

- YouTube video descriptions
- Social media bio
- Blog posts about earning money online
- Forums and communities
- Messaging groups

### Tips for Success

1. **Create content** about MRShort to attract interested users
2. **Be honest** about the platform - realistic expectations lead to better retention
3. **Help your referrals** - their success is your success!
4. **Stay active** - an active referrer inspires active referrals

## Referral Rules

- Self-referrals are not allowed
- Creating fake accounts is prohibited
- Commission is calculated on valid clicks only
- Referral fraud may result in account termination

## FAQ

### When do I get paid?

Referral earnings are added to your balance in real-time. Withdraw them anytime once you meet the minimum.

### Is there a limit to referrals?

No! You can refer unlimited users.

### What if my referral stops using MRShort?

Your commission is based on their active earnings. If they're inactive, you won't earn from them during that period.
