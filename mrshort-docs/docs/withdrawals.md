# Withdrawals

Learn how to withdraw your earnings from MRShort.

## Minimum Withdrawal Amounts

| Payment Method | Minimum | Processing Time |
|---------------|---------|-----------------|
| PayPal | $5.00 | 1-3 business days |
| Bitcoin (BTC) | $5.00 | Within 24 hours |
| USDT (TRC20) | $5.00 | Within 24 hours |
| Bank Transfer | $50.00 | 3-5 business days |
| Payoneer | $20.00 | 2-4 business days |

## How to Withdraw

1. Navigate to **Withdrawals** in the sidebar
2. Check your available balance
3. Select your preferred payment method
4. Enter the withdrawal amount
5. Confirm your payment details
6. Click **Request Withdrawal**

## Payment Details Setup

Before your first withdrawal, you need to set up your payment details:

### PayPal
1. Go to **Settings → Payment Settings**
2. Enter your PayPal email address
3. Save changes

### Cryptocurrency
1. Go to **Settings → Payment Settings**
2. Enter your wallet address
3. Double-check the address (crypto transactions are irreversible!)
4. Save changes

### Bank Transfer
1. Go to **Settings → Payment Settings**
2. Enter your bank details:
   - Bank name
   - Account holder name
   - IBAN/Account number
   - SWIFT code
3. Save changes

## Withdrawal Status

Track your withdrawal status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Request submitted, awaiting review |
| **Processing** | Payment is being processed |
| **Completed** | Payment sent successfully |
| **Rejected** | Request was declined (check email for reason) |

## Common Issues

### Why was my withdrawal rejected?

Common reasons:
- Invalid payment details
- Account verification needed
- Suspicious activity detected
- Minimum amount not met

### How long does it take?

Processing times vary by method. Cryptocurrency is fastest (usually same day), while bank transfers take the longest.

### Can I cancel a withdrawal?

Only **Pending** withdrawals can be cancelled. Once processing begins, it cannot be cancelled.

## Tips

:::tip Save on Fees
Cryptocurrency withdrawals often have lower fees than traditional methods.
:::

:::warning Verify Your Details
Always double-check your payment details before submitting. Incorrect details may result in lost funds.
:::
