# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about MRShort.

## Account & Registration

### How do I create an account?
Visit [mrshort.io](https://mrshort.io), click Sign Up, and follow the registration process. You'll need to verify your email address.

### Can I have multiple accounts?
No, each person is allowed only one account. Multiple accounts may result in suspension.

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

---

## Links & Shortening

### How many links can I create?
There's no limit on the number of links you can create.

### Can I customize my short link?
Yes! When shortening a link, you can set a custom alias instead of using the random one.

### Can I edit a link after creating it?
Yes, you can edit the destination URL and other settings from your Links page.

### What happens if I delete a link?
The short URL will stop working and show a 404 error.

---

## Earnings & Payments

### How much can I earn per click?
Earnings vary by country. Check the CPM rates in your Reports section for current rates.

### When can I withdraw?
You can withdraw once you reach the minimum threshold ($5 for most methods).

### Why wasn't my click counted?
Clicks may not count if:
- You clicked your own link
- The visitor didn't complete the ad process
- Traffic appears to be from bots or proxies
- Same IP clicked within 24 hours

### How long do withdrawals take?
- PayPal: 1-3 business days
- Crypto: Within 24 hours
- Bank Transfer: 3-5 business days

## Technical

### Is there an API?
Yes! Check our [API Documentation](./api) for details.

### Does MRShort work with AdBlock users?
Visitors with ad blockers may be asked to disable them to access the destination link.
